What is a Word Counter?

A word counter is a tool that counts the number of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in a block of text. It is useful for writers, students, bloggers and anyone who needs to track text length.

How to Use

Simply paste or type your text into the text area above. The word count, character count, sentence count, paragraph count and estimated reading time will update instantly as you type.

Why Use This Word Counter?

  • Instant results — counts update as you type
  • Completely free with no sign-up required
  • Works offline — all processing happens in your browser
  • Includes reading time estimate based on average reading speed
