What is a Character Counter?

A character counter counts the exact number of characters in your text, including or excluding spaces. It helps you stay within character limits for social media posts, meta descriptions, SMS messages and more.

How to Use

Paste or type your text in the text area above. Character counts update in real time. Check the social media limits section to see if your text fits popular platform requirements.
