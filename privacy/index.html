Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Geeta Tech Hub ("I", "me") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how data is handled when you use this website and its tools.

Browser-Based Tools

Most tools on Geeta Tech Hub process data entirely in your web browser. When a tool is marked as "processed locally" or "browser-based", your files and data are not uploaded to any server. They remain on your device throughout the process.

Data Collection

Geeta Tech Hub does not require user accounts, login or registration. I do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it through the contact form.

When you submit a contact form, the following data is collected:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content

This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

Analytics

The website may use basic analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand traffic patterns. This analytics data is anonymous and does not track individual users.

Cookies

Geeta Tech Hub does not use tracking cookies. Essential cookies may be set by hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) for security and performance purposes.

Third-Party Services

The website uses Google Fonts for typography. Google may collect anonymous usage data as described in their privacy policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
